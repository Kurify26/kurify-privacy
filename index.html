Kurify Privacy Policy

Kurify provides tools for Kuri owners and members to manage group, installment, bid, and payment information. This policy describes how Kurify handles information when you use the app.

Privacy contact: kurify26@gmail.com.

Information handled by Kurify

  • Account and profile information: name, mobile number, account identifiers, and, where provided, an email address, alternate mobile number, address, or UPI ID.
  • Kuri activity: group membership, installment schedules, amounts, collections, bids, payment records, and receipts. A Kuri owner may enter information about members they add.
  • Group messages: messages sent in a Kuri's group chat, together with the sender and time.
  • Authentication information: information used to authenticate accounts, including passkey hashes, session identifiers, and information from a linked sign-in provider where used.
  • Support communications: information you choose to provide when you contact Kurify.

To confirm: any additional device information, IP addresses, diagnostic logs, or analytics collected by Kurify or its service providers.

How information is used

Kurify uses this information to create and secure accounts, provide Kuri and member features, display schedules and payment records, support group chat, generate receipts, and respond to support requests.

When information is shared

  • Relevant Kuri owners and authorized members can see information made available to them by the app. Group chat messages are visible to other authorized participants in that chat.
  • Supabase provides backend services used by Kurify. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that provider and Supabase process the information needed for sign-in under their own practices.
  • If you choose a UPI app, Kurify passes the owner's UPI ID and name, the amount, and a payment note to that app. The payment takes place in the selected UPI app; the Kuri owner records its receipt in Kurify. The UPI app and payment providers may keep their own records under their practices; deleting a Kurify account does not itself delete those external records.
  • If you print or share a receipt, its contents are sent to the service or recipient you select.

Kurify does not sell personal data or share it with advertising partners. The app does not show ads.

To confirm: all service-provider settings and any legal or other disclosures that may apply.

Device storage and security

Kurify connects to its backend using HTTPS and uses device secure storage for session information. If you use biometric unlock, your device performs the biometric check; Kurify receives the authentication result rather than your fingerprint or face image.

To confirm: the backend, backup, access-control, and operational security practices before making any additional security commitments.

Retention and deletion

Proposed approach: After a verified deletion request, Kurify intends to delete the account and associated data, including financial data, except where specific information needs to be retained for a documented, legitimate reason, such as security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Any exception would be limited to the information needed and disclosed with its reason and retention period. This approach has not yet been implemented or verified.

Proposed retention: Kuri records are intended to be kept in Kurify's live database for 5 years after the Kuri's final due date. They would then be moved to a backup database for 3 more years and permanently deleted afterwards. If a verified account-deletion request is made, Kurify intends to delete the associated data from both the live and backup databases, except for specific records that must be kept for a documented security, fraud-prevention, or legal reason, which would be disclosed with the reason and period.

Required before final publication: confirm these periods with a qualified Indian adviser; state the retention for accounts and chat messages that are not tied to a Kuri; confirm that the live and backup databases actually apply these rules; and verify how deletion is carried out in production.

The proposed manual request process is described on the account-deletion draft page. It has been tested in a separate non-production project using fictional records, but production settings and the full retention process still need to be verified before this policy is finalized.
