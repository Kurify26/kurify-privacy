Request Kurify account deletion

Last updated: 3 November 2026. You can ask Kurify to delete your account and associated data without signing in or reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email kurify26@gmail.com with the subject "Kurify account deletion request."
  2. Include your name, the mobile number registered to your Kurify account, and whether you are a Kuri owner or member. Never include your passkey or other login secrets.
  3. Kurify verifies your request. We first send a one-time code (OTP) to the account's registered mobile number. If you no longer have that number, we ask for details only the account holder would know, such as your name and Kuri names, and, for members, may confirm with the relevant Kuri owner. We may decline a request we cannot verify.

Kurify aims to confirm receipt promptly and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

After verification, Kurify deletes your account and associated data, including profile details, authentication information, group chat messages, and Kuri and payment records, from both the live and backup databases.

What may be kept

Specific records may be kept only where required for a documented security, fraud-prevention, or legal reason. Any such exception is limited to the information needed, and we will tell you what is kept, why, and for how long. Records held by UPI apps, payment providers, or other people, such as a Kuri owner or a recipient of a receipt, are outside Kurify's control.

Retention if you do not request deletion

Kuri records and group chat messages are kept for 5 years after the Kuri's final due date, then moved to a backup database for 3 more years and permanently deleted. See the privacy policy for details.
