Request Kurify account deletion

Kurify plans to accept account-deletion requests by email. This process must be verified before it is offered to users.

Proposed approach: After verifying a request, Kurify intends to delete the account and associated data, including financial data, except where specific information needs to be retained for a documented, legitimate reason, such as security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Any exception would be limited to the information needed and disclosed with its reason and retention period. This approach has not yet been implemented or verified.

  1. Email kurify26@gmail.com with the subject "Kurify account deletion request."
  2. Tell support how to identify your Kurify account. Never include your passkey or other login secrets in the email.
  3. Kurify will need to verify account ownership before processing the request. The verification procedure and expected response time must be confirmed.
Required before final publication: identify the account, profile, authentication, chat, financial, and backup data that will be deleted; any data that must be retained, why and for how long; how requests will be verified and fulfilled; and the expected timeframe.

Operator checklist

  • Put an obvious link to this page in the app's account options.
  • Make the page usable without signing in or reinstalling the app.
  • Establish and test the manual workflow for both owners and members, including Supabase, linked authentication, shared records, and backups.
  • Confirm the retention policy and communicate any legally required exceptions to users.
