Request Kurify account deletion

Kurify plans to accept account-deletion requests by email. The proposed manual workflow has been tested in a separate non-production project using fictional records. Production settings and the full retention process have not yet been verified.

Proposed approach: After verifying a request through an existing verified phone number or email address, Kurify intends to delete the account and associated data, including financial data, except where specific information needs to be retained for a documented, legitimate reason, such as security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Any exception would be limited to the information needed and disclosed with its reason and retention period. Kurify is considering a 30-day completion target, but has not committed to or verified that timeframe. Deletion is intended to cover both live and backup databases. Otherwise, Kuri records are intended to be kept for 5 years after the Kuri's final due date, moved to a backup database for 3 more years, and then permanently deleted.

  1. Email kurify26@gmail.com with the subject "Kurify account deletion request."
  2. Tell support how to identify your Kurify account. Never include your passkey or other login secrets in the email.
  3. Support will need to verify account ownership before processing the request. The procedure for people who can no longer access their verified contact method still needs to be established.
Required before final publication: identify the account, profile, authentication, chat, financial, and backup data that will be deleted; confirm the 5-year and 3-year periods with a qualified Indian adviser; verify how requests will be verified and fulfilled in production; and confirm the response timeframe.
